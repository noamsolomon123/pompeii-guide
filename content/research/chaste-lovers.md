# Insula of the Chaste Lovers (Casa dei Casti Amanti) — Stop Research

## Identity

- **English name:** Insula of the Chaste Lovers / House of the Chaste Lovers
- **Italian name:** Casa dei Casti Amanti / Insula dei Casti Amanti
- **Address / catalog code:** Regio IX, Insula 12, no. 6 (IX.12.6) — [Wikidata Q27688613](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q27688613); confirmed by the Pompeii Archaeological Park's own site page, which labels it "(Insula 12, no. 6)" — [pompeiisites.org, Insula of the Lovers Chaste](https://pompeiisites.org/en/archaeological-site/insula-of-the-lovers-chaste/)
- **GPS:** 40.7509, 14.4900 (approx. centroid of the insula). Two independent sources agree closely: Wikidata gives 40°45'3.28"N, 14°29'24.25"E = 40.750912, 14.490069 ([Wikidata Q27688613](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q27688613)); OpenStreetMap node 5010108076 gives 40.750938, 14.489956 ([OSM node 5010108076](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/5010108076)). Both are approximate building-footprint points, not a single doorway pin.
- **Regio:** IX. NOTE ON SCOPE: the named house/insula itself is Insula 12, but the two headline 2023 discoveries (the "pizza" fresco and the bakery-prison) were officially sited by the Park in the **adjoining Insula 10, Regio IX** — verbatim from the Park's own press release: "The fresco has been found in the atrium of the house in Insula 10, Regio IX, that is now being excavated" ([pompeiisites.org press release, 27 June 2023](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-a-still-life-discovered-by-the-new-excavations-of-regio-ix/)) and "Region IX, Insula 10" for the prison-bakery ([pompeiisites.org press release, 8 December 2023](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/)). However, the Park's own 2026 update on the same dig explicitly calls the whole dig the "Casti Amanti complex," describing the bread-making room as connected via "adjoining stables" to the same production complex as the named house ([pompeiisites.org, equid skeleton update](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/new-investigations-in-the-house-of-the-chaste-lovers-the-excavation-of-a-room-used-for-bread-making-has-unearthed-the-skeleton-of-an-equid/)). In practice, guides and press treat "Insula/House of the Chaste Lovers" as the umbrella name for this whole excavation zone (Insulae 10 and 12 together); this file follows that usage but flags the precise addresses fact-by-fact.
- **Opening hours — the 10:30 claim, VERIFIED:** The Park's official "open buildings" hours list gives this exact entry: **"Insula of the Lovers Chaste (Regio IX) — Opening times: 10:30 – 18:20, last entrance 18:00"** — this is the latest opening time of any listed building in the park, confirming the brief's claim ([pompeiisites.org, Houses/Open Buildings list](https://pompeiisites.org/en/houses/), fetched September 2026). By contrast, the main archaeological park gates open at 9:00 year-round ([pompeiisites.org, Timetables and Tickets](https://pompeiisites.org/en/visiting-info/timetables-and-tickets/)).
- **IMPORTANT CAVEAT for the 20 Oct 2026 visit date:** the 10:30–18:20 figure above was captured while the park was still on its summer schedule (summer season runs 16 March–14 October; the general park then closes at 19:00/last entry 17:30). From 15 October the whole park shifts to winter hours — main gates 9:00–17:00, last entry 15:30 ([pompeiisites.org, Timetables and Tickets](https://pompeiisites.org/en/visiting-info/timetables-and-tickets/)). No official source found states whether this specific insula's 10:30 opening / later closing time shifts on the same winter schedule. **Flag this clearly in the audio guide as an optional bonus stop with a confirmed LATE opening relative to the main gates, and tell visitors to re-check the exact hours at pompeiisites.org or at the entrance info-point (tel. +39 081 8575 347, email pompei.info@cultura.gov.it) shortly before the 20 Oct 2026 visit**, since winter re-scheduling of this specific building was not confirmed in any source found.
- Entry is via the general Pompeii ticket; no separate ticket was found required for this insula, but it is listed as a discretely-timed "open building" rather than an always-open street, so it can be closed outside its stated window even while the rest of the park is open.

## What you see

1. **The "chaste kiss" fresco** — a Fourth-Style wall painting in a triclinium showing banquet scenes, one of which depicts a couple exchanging a kiss; this is what gave the whole insula its name. In-situ. ([pompeiisites.org, Insula of the Lovers Chaste](https://pompeiisites.org/en/archaeological-site/insula-of-the-lovers-chaste/))
2. **The "pizza" still-life fresco** — found in the atrium of a house in the adjoining Insula 10, announced 27 June 2023: a silver tray holding a flat focaccia-type bread topped with fruit (pomegranate, likely dates), spices, a possible dollop of moretum (an ancient pesto-like paste), plus a wine cup and dried fruit. In-situ at time of discovery. ([pompeiisites.org press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-a-still-life-discovered-by-the-new-excavations-of-regio-ix/))
3. **Small, high, barred windows in the bakery** — iron-barred openings placed high on the walls, positioned to admit minimal light while blocking escape; announced 8 December 2023. In-situ. ([pompeiisites.org press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/))
4. **Four millstones set tightly together** on a volcanic-stone (basalt) floor. In-situ. ([pompeiisites.org press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/))
5. **Semicircular grooves/furrows cut into the basalt paving** around the millstones — physical evidence of the repetitive circular walking path forced on the donkeys (and the workers alongside them) turning the mills. In-situ. ([pompeiisites.org press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/))
6. **No street-facing doors** — the bakery-prison area and its attached stable had no direct access to the street, only an interior route through the house's atrium, meaning anyone working there could not leave unsupervised. Structural evidence, in-situ. ([pompeiisites.org press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/))
7. **Skeletal remains context (not generally viewable in-situ):** the skeletons of three human victims were found in the work rooms beside the oven (reported June 2023, still being discussed in the December 2023 release), and in a 2026 update the Park announced the skeleton of an equid (donkey/mule-type animal) found in the bread-making room. Both finds are described as undergoing laboratory study by an interdisciplinary team (archaeology, archaeozoology, archaeobotany, anthropology), with results due in the Park's own e-journal — i.e., **under study, not confirmed on public display**, rather than removed to the Naples Archaeological Museum (MANN); no MANN transfer was mentioned in any official source found. ([pompeiisites.org, still-life press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-a-still-life-discovered-by-the-new-excavations-of-regio-ix/); [pompeiisites.org, prison-bakery press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/); [pompeiisites.org, equid update](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/new-investigations-in-the-house-of-the-chaste-lovers-the-excavation-of-a-room-used-for-bread-making-has-unearthed-the-skeleton-of-an-equid/))
8. **The "House of the Painters at Work"** (within the same insula complex) — unfinished preparatory fresco sketches, work interrupted mid-stroke, apparently by the AD 79 eruption during a renovation possibly tied to earthquake repair. In-situ. ([pompeiisites.org, Insula of the Lovers Chaste](https://pompeiisites.org/en/archaeological-site/insula-of-the-lovers-chaste/))

## History in layers

### 30-second intro
This elegant house takes its name from a fresco of a kissing couple, uncovered during a 1980s excavation campaign. Attached to the residence is a working bakery that, renewed digs in 2023–2025 revealed, doubled as a locked workspace for enslaved laborers and pack animals grinding grain. In the same excavation zone, a still-life fresco resembling a modern pizza went viral worldwide in 2023 — though it isn't actually pizza.

### 2-minute story (~150 words)
The house takes its name (Casti Amanti, "Chaste Lovers") from a Fourth-Style fresco of an embracing couple in its triclinium, uncovered during an excavation campaign that the Park dates to 1912 and then 1982–2010 — the insula was "not yet entirely investigated" even after those campaigns ([pompeiisites.org, Insula of the Lovers Chaste](https://pompeiisites.org/en/archaeological-site/insula-of-the-lovers-chaste/)). Starting in 2023, renewed digs in the same Regio IX zone (the neighboring Insula 10) uncovered an attached bakery whose small barred windows and doorless, atrium-only access pointed to a space used to confine enslaved workers and mill animals — announced 8 December 2023 ([pompeiisites.org](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/)). Months earlier, on 27 June 2023, the Park had announced a still-life fresco resembling a flatbread, which international media quickly nicknamed "the pizza fresco" ([pompeiisites.org](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-a-still-life-discovered-by-the-new-excavations-of-regio-ix/)). Both finds sit under the ash layer from the AD 79 eruption of Vesuvius, and both are part of the Park's ongoing "new excavations of Regio IX" — proof that, after nearly 2,000 years, Pompeii is still yielding fresh discoveries almost yearly.

### Deep-dive (~250 words)
The bakery-prison is the more sobering find. Excavators found small, high windows fitted with iron bars — designed, in Park director Gabriel Zuchtriegel's words, to reflect "the most shocking side of ancient slavery," their bars showing "brute violence" ([pompeiisites.org press release, 8 December 2023](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/)). Four millstones stood close together on a basalt floor scored with semicircular furrows — grooves that channeled the endless circular walk of donkeys and the enslaved people paired with them, one animal and one person per mill, per the Park's account. Crucially, the space had no door onto the street: the only way in or out ran through the house's atrium, meaning movement was controlled by the household. Archaeologists drew an explicit literary parallel to the 2nd-century AD novel Metamorphoses (The Golden Ass) by Apuleius, which describes enslaved millers and donkeys grinding grain under similarly grim conditions — a rare case of a literary source matching a physical excavation almost exactly.

The "pizza" fresco, announced the same year in the neighboring house, belongs to a well-documented ancient genre called Xenia — still-life paintings of hospitality gifts, of which some 300 examples are known from the Vesuvian cities. The Park's own release was explicit that the image "obviously can't" be pizza, since tomatoes and mozzarella are absent — ingredients unavailable in the Roman world. Both finds, and the equid skeleton reported later, model how Pompeii's "new excavations" work today: conservation-led digs combined with archaeozoology, archaeobotany, and anthropology, run as ongoing science rather than one-off treasure hunts, with results published in the Park's e-journal over time.

## The detail most visitors miss

Look down, not just at the walls: the basalt paving around the four millstones carries **semicircular grooves worn or cut into the stone**, tracing the exact circular path that the donkeys — and the enslaved workers paired beside them — were forced to walk, over and over, to turn the mills ([pompeiisites.org press release, 8 December 2023](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/)). It's easy to focus on the famous kissing fresco or the "pizza" image and miss that the floor itself is a physical record of forced, repetitive labor. No official source found specifies a dedicated raised walkway for this insula, so viewing conditions may be from ground level along the excavation edge — check the current park map at the entrance for the best vantage point on the visit day.

## Anecdotes

1. **The 2003 fresco theft.** Visitors stole two frescoes off a wall inside the House of the Chaste Lovers; the theft damaged several other paintings in the process, and Pompeii's camera system had reportedly been out of service for months beforehand. The two stolen frescoes were recovered some months later, though other pieces taken from the site over the years have never been returned. ([Wikipedia, Conservation issues of Pompeii and Herculaneum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_issues_of_Pompeii_and_Herculaneum))
2. **The global "pizza" headline storm — DISPUTED FRAMING.** When the Park announced the still-life fresco on 27 June 2023, outlets worldwide ran it as a "2,000-year-old pizza." The Park's own release pre-empted this, stating plainly that the image "can't" be pizza because it lacks tomatoes and mozzarella — this is a case of media shorthand outrunning the archaeology; the correct description is a Xenia-genre hospitality still life of a flatbread/focaccia with fruit and wine. ([pompeiisites.org press release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-a-still-life-discovered-by-the-new-excavations-of-regio-ix/))
3. **A 2,000-year-old novel matched the dig.** When archaeologists described the bakery's confinement setup, they pointed to a real 2nd-century AD text, Apuleius's Metamorphoses (The Golden Ass), which describes enslaved people yoked alongside donkeys grinding grain in comparable conditions — a rare direct match between Roman literature and a physical excavation. ([pompeiisites.org press release, 8 December 2023](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/))

## FAQ

**Q: Is that really a pizza in the fresco?**
No — this is confirmed directly by the Pompeii Archaeological Park itself, not just outside commentary. It's a Xenia-style still life of a flatbread/focaccia topped with fruit and served on a silver tray with a wine cup; true pizza requires tomatoes and mozzarella, and tomatoes are a New World crop that did not reach Italy until well over a thousand years after this fresco was painted. ([pompeiisites.org](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-a-still-life-discovered-by-the-new-excavations-of-regio-ix/))

**Q: What time does this stop actually open?**
Per the Park's own published hours (checked September 2026), the Insula of the Lovers Chaste opens at 10:30, later than the main gates (9:00), with last entrance 18:00 and closing 18:20. This is a summer-season figure; confirm current hours close to the 20 Oct 2026 visit date, since the park's general winter schedule (from 15 October) runs 9:00–17:00 and it isn't confirmed whether this building's hours shift the same way. ([pompeiisites.org, Houses/Open Buildings](https://pompeiisites.org/en/houses/); [pompeiisites.org, Timetables and Tickets](https://pompeiisites.org/en/visiting-info/timetables-and-tickets/))

**Q: Can I see the skeletons of the enslaved workers or the mill animals?**
The human remains (three victims, reported 2023) and an equid skeleton (reported in a 2026 update) found in this bakery complex are described by the Park as being analyzed by a multidisciplinary lab team, with findings due to be published over time in the Park's e-journal — no official source confirms them as currently on public in-situ display. Related themes were explored in the Park's "The Other Pompeii: Ordinary Lives in the Shadow of Vesuvius" exhibition, which opened 15 December 2023 at the Palestra Grande. ([pompeiisites.org, prison-bakery release](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-prison-bakery-emerges/); [pompeiisites.org, equid update](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/new-investigations-in-the-house-of-the-chaste-lovers-the-excavation-of-a-room-used-for-bread-making-has-unearthed-the-skeleton-of-an-equid/))

**Q: Where exactly is this relative to the rest of the site?**
The named house (Casa dei Casti Amanti) sits at Regio IX, Insula 12, no. 6. The two most famous 2023 discoveries — the "pizza" fresco and the bakery-prison — were officially located by the Park in the neighboring Insula 10, Regio IX, as part of the same continuous "new excavations" zone that the Park itself refers to collectively as the "Casti Amanti complex." ([Wikidata Q27688613](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q27688613); [pompeiisites.org press releases, above](https://pompeiisites.org/en/comunicati/pompeii-a-still-life-discovered-by-the-new-excavations-of-regio-ix/))

## Hebrew naming

בעברית הבית מכונה בדרך כלל "בית הנאהבים הטהורים" או בתעתיק ישיר "קאזה דיי קסטי אמנטי" (Casa dei Casti Amanti), ולעיתים גם "האינסולה של הנאהבים הטהורים" (Insula of the Chaste Lovers). השם ניתן בעקבות פרסקו של זוג המתנשק בטריקליניום (חדר סעודות), שהתגלה במהלך חפירות בשנים 1982–2010.

האזור כולו שייך ל"רג'יו התשיעי" (Regio IX) של פומפיי — אחד מאזורי החפירות החדשות והמשמעותיות ביותר בשנים 2023–2025.

מונח מקצועי חשוב: "פיסטרינום" (pistrinum) — המונח הלטיני לבית מאפייה-טחנה רומי, שבו טחנו תבואה באמצעות ריחיים מסתובבים שהופעלו בכוח חמורים ועבדים. הפיסטרינום שהתגלה כאן שימש, כפי הנראה, גם כמקום כליאה לעבדים ולבעלי החיים שהפעילו את הריחיים — בזכות חלונות קטנים ומסורגים שהתגלו בשנת 2023.

הפרסקו שכונה בתקשורת העולמית "פיצה בת 2000 שנה" (בשנת 2023) הוא למעשה "נתורת מורתא" (natura morta, ציור דומם) מהסוג הקרוי "קסניה" (Xenia) — ציור של מזון ארוח, ולא פיצה של ממש, שכן העגבנייה הגיעה לאיטליה רק כאלף שנה מאוחר יותר. חשוב להדגיש זאת במדריך השמע ולא להציג את התיאור התקשורתי כעובדה ארכיאולוגית.

מנהל הפארק הארכיאולוגי של פומפיי, גבריאל צוכטריגל (Gabriel Zuchtriegel), הוא הדובר הציבורי המרכזי של הממצאים הללו בהודעות הרשמיות.
